02 / How I work

Own the outcome.
Understand the tradeoffs.

01

Start with the behavior.

Connect the business need to the data, decisions, and workflows the system must support. TurfTrigger’s shared rules and plan checks grew out of that need for consistency.

02

Build with AI agents.

I used agentic engineering throughout TurfTrigger: coding, research, design, troubleshooting, and testing. I am the sole project owner and human contributor, directing the work and owning the service.

03

Check the result.

Use regression tests, historical replay, completion monitoring, and release verification to examine actual behavior. Document what those checks establish and what remains untested.
