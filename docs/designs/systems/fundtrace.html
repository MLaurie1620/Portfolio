A shorter path to evidence.

01 / My contribution

I created the end-to-end review workflow.

I am FundTrace’s sole creator and project owner, responsible for archive processing, AI scoring, evidence output, and evaluation. The tool helps auditors find evidence of vendor rebates, allowances, and funding agreements in large email archives.

FundTrace removes exact duplicates, groups replies into conversations, and uses AI to score each exchange. Shortlisted conversations include a summary and supporting quotations. Auditors make the final decision, and their corrections are retained.

Email archive↓Deduplicate + group conversations↓AI scoring + evidence quotations↓Auditor review + recorded corrections

02 / Evaluation discipline

Measure the workflow that people receive.

Development and testing covered seven vendor archives. Separate scorer, reviewer, and optimizer model calls examined mistakes and proposed instruction changes. Vendor-level test separation exposed generalization problems that same-vendor testing had hidden.

Experiments compared revised instructions and input context against the deployed baseline. The evaluation tracks both relevant conversations found and the review workload created, so a larger shortlist cannot pass as an unqualified improvement.

On the same archive, providing full email text increased recall to 95% while flagging 49% of conversations. Larger instruction sets mainly shifted the balance between recall and reading workload, without a clear overall improvement.

October 2026 internal evaluation, reported in the project summary. These results describe one vendor benchmark, which was later reused for development. Fresh vendor testing is needed to assess broader performance; review-time savings remain illustrative.

04 / Engineering judgment

Make failures visible before tuning further.

Evaluation exposed silent failures in the reviewer stage and zero scores being stored as blank values. Both were corrected, with failures recorded explicitly.

The next validation step is a shared relevance rubric, blinded auditor labeling, and testing on fresh vendors. That separates ambiguous labels, missing context, and model error before further optimization.

Skills demonstrated by the project: Workflow automation, AI orchestration, evidence traceability, evaluation design, failure observability, and communicating operational tradeoffs.
